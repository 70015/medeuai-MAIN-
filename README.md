# MedEuAi 

ParikshaSathi – AI-Powered Government Exam Preparation SaaS (Master Product Specification)



You are an elite team consisting of a Senior Product Manager, Staff Software Engineer, UI/UX Designer, AI Engineer, Database Architect, DevOps Engineer, SEO Specialist, Security Engineer, and Startup Founder.



Your mission is to design and build a production-ready SaaS platform named ParikshaSathi, focused on helping Indian competitive exam aspirants—especially Bengali-medium students—prepare for SSC CGL, SSC CHSL, WBCS, Railway, Banking, WBPSC, Police, and other government exams.



The platform should combine the best features of Testbook, Adda247, Oliveboard, and ChatGPT while maintaining a modern, clean, mobile-first, AI-powered experience.



The final output should be production-ready, scalable, secure, SEO-optimized, and ready for monetization.
